⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida — is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. After the mission Down to Earth, look for Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning: when she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept her invitation. Finish that balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1. Everything else can still be earned in free roam after the story. Sources: KeenGamer’s trophy guide; PowerPyx and GTABase, via EGamers.

Also: cheats disable trophies and saving while active, and there is no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. (KeenGamer; GTABase and TechWiser, via EGamers.)

About this guide

Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them — the list in this guide matches the official 55 (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden) as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry and cites its sources beside it: a named guide or a link, so you can check each fact where it came from. Nothing here is copied from another guide, and the guide shows no Rockstar logo, official artwork, box art or game screenshot — the look is our own, drawn in code.

Sources consulted include KeenGamer, PowerPyx, Push Square, GamesFuze, EGamers, GameTrek, DamnModz, GTA6Database, TheGamesWiki, GamerBlurb, AlcastHQ, Of Zen and Computing, PlayHub and TrueAchievements. Where guides disagree or a method is still unconfirmed, this guide says so instead of guessing.
